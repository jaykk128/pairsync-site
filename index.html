Voice Memo Sync

An Android app that copies your voice recordings to a folder in your own Google Drive.

  • Runs only on your phone. There is no Voice Memo Sync server.
  • Uploads straight to your Google Drive, with no file size limit, resuming where it left off if the connection drops.
  • Can see only the folder and files it creates in your Drive.
  • Upload only: it never deletes anything, on your phone or in your Drive.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Voice Memo Sync is a personal app and is not offered on Google Play.
