Pairsync

An Android app that keeps folders on your phone in step with folders in your own Google Drive.

  • Pair any phone folder with any Drive folder: Voice Recorder to a "Voice Memos" folder in one tap, or anything else.
  • Seven sync methods: two way, upload only, upload mirror, upload then delete, download only, download mirror, download then delete.
  • Runs only on your phone and talks to Drive directly. There is no Pairsync server.
  • No file size limit. Large files resume where they left off if the connection drops.
  • Deletions go to a trash, Drive's or the folder's own, so they can be undone for 30 days.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Pairsync is not offered on Google Play yet.
