Voice Memo Sync Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Generic Shell, LLC, which operates Voice Memo Sync ("Voice Memo Sync," "we," "us," or "our"), handles information in connection with the Voice Memo Sync Android app and this website (collectively, the "Services").

The short version: Voice Memo Sync runs only on your phone. It copies your voice recordings from your phone to your own Google Drive. There is no Voice Memo Sync server, and we do not receive, store, or see your recordings or any other information about you.

Scope

This Policy applies to everyone who uses the Services.

Information the App Uses

The app uses only what it needs to work, and all of it stays on your phone or goes to your own Google account:

  • Your recordings: with Android's permission to read audio files, the app reads the recordings in the folder you choose (by default Samsung Voice Recorder's folder) and uploads them.
  • Your Google account: the email address of the Google account you connect, kept on your phone so the app asks for access to the same account each time.
  • Google Drive access: the app asks only for permission to see and change the files it creates itself (Google's "drive.file" scope). It creates one folder and uploads your recordings into it. It cannot see any other files in your Drive. Google Play services on your phone holds the access tokens; the app does not store them.
  • Upload records: a list of the recordings the app has seen, with their name, size, date, and upload status, plus your settings. These stay on your phone.

What we do not collect: we receive nothing. The app has no account with us, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting to us, and no third-party code other than Google Play services for connecting your Google account. It does not use your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

How Information Is Used

The app uses the information above only to upload your recordings to your Google Drive and to show you what it has uploaded. Nothing is used for advertising, sold, or used to train AI models.

Google User Data

Voice Memo Sync's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your recordings travel directly from your phone to Google over an encrypted connection. Google stores them in your Drive under the Google Privacy Policy.

Data Storage and Security

The app's records stay in its private storage on your phone, protected by Android's app sandbox, and are left out of Android backups. Uploads use HTTPS only.

Sharing of Information

We do not share information, because we do not receive any. Your recordings are shared only with Google, as your Drive provider, when the app uploads them at your direction.

Retention

  • On your phone: until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • In your Google Drive: until you delete the files. The app only uploads. It never deletes anything, on your phone or in your Drive.

Your Choices

  • Stop uploads and revoke access: in the app, Settings, Disconnect. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Choose what uploads: pick the folder, the start date for older recordings, Wi-Fi only, and plugged in only, in the app.
  • Remove everything from your phone: uninstall the app.

Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the new version here and change the Last Updated date.
